# Community guidelines

Treat contributors with respect. Constructive disagreement is welcome;
harassment, threats, personal attacks and disclosure of private information are
not. Focus reviews on behavior and code, and give people room to learn.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions,
and restrict participation when needed. Maintainers are held to the same
standard. Use GitHub's report-abuse tools for conduct that violates GitHub's
community rules. Do not post private incident details in a public issue.
