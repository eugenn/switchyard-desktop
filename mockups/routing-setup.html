Stage router roles

Every turn begins at Efficient and escalates to Capable only when Switchyard sees low confidence. These roles are fixed.

1 Efficient Tiny, localized edits, quick syntax fixes, short completions and narrowly scoped diagnostics.
2 Capable Architecture, hard debugging, cross-module changes and ambiguous, high-value work needing strong judgment.

Stage routing has no classifier target and no fallback target — the escalation rule replaces both. Switching back to the classifier restores its own role list.
